# References and attribution

1. Luis A. Medina and Armin Straub, *On multiple and infinite log-concavity*, [arXiv:1405.1765v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.1765v1), 7 May 2014; *Annals of Combinatorics* 20 (2016), 125–138. Conjecture 5.2 supplies the general positive-polynomial question; Example 5.3 supplies the sharper trinomial threshold 10. Lemma 2.5 and Example 5.1 provide the factor-cone context and the warning against an unsupported multiplication argument.
2. Matthew H. Y. Xie, Candice X. T. Zhang and Philip B. Zhang, *A unified approach to infinite log-concavity of combinatorial sequences*, [arXiv:2610.12237v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12237v1), 8 October 2026. Section 2, especially Lemmas 2.1–2.2, Theorem 2.3 and Corollaries 2.5–2.6, supplies the local closure and shrinking-disk framework. The paper also credits related earlier work of Liu and Mao. These tools are prior work and are rederived in the manuscript with conservative constants; they are not claimed as newly invented methods.
3. Peter R. W. McNamara and Bruce E. Sagan, *Infinite log-concavity: developments and conjectures*, [arXiv:0808.1065v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0808.1065v2); *Advances in Applied Mathematics* 44 (2010), 1–15. This is background for the factor-cone and legacy determinant framework, not an unproved dependency of the new A–C arguments.
4. *Supplementary exact computational records for trinomial log-concavity* (2026). The baseline/ directory contains the finite coefficient/interval data and its original verifier. These records are a data reference, not a claim of a separate published paper.

The manuscript preserves the exact mathematical attribution and the distinction between established tools and their application to positive-polynomial powers. No third-party PDF is distributed here, and this reference list does not certify global priority.
